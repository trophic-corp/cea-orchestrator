Note on Scope

This is Phase D of a six-part strategic report, covering Objective 5
(Bill of Materials and Raw Material Sourcing). It provides
component-level sourcing guidance for the products defined in Phase C,
organized into seven functional groups rather than one undifferentiated
list, for usability. Sourcing priority follows the brief\'s stated order
--- Indian suppliers first, then Taiwan/Japan/South Korea, then
Europe/USA, with China used only where genuinely unavoidable --- and
every entry states explicitly whether that priority could be honored or
not, and why.

8\. Sourcing Priority Summary

8.1 Genuinely India-First Categories

Aluminum extrusion and fabrication, stainless steel fabrication,
fasteners, bearings, powder coating/anodizing, circulation pumps,
solenoid valves (standard grade), filters, tubing, fertilizers, coco
coir substrate, NFT trays, and saffron corms can all be sourced
domestically today without compromising on quality --- several of these
(coco coir substrate and pumps in particular) are genuine Indian
manufacturing export strengths, not just adequate substitutes.

8.2 Assemble Locally on Imported Components

LED fixtures, sensor nodes, PLC/HMI-based controllers, and dosing
systems should be designed and assembled in Coimbatore using imported
semiconductor/sensor/diode components (per Phase C\'s manufacturing
feasibility conclusions) --- this is standard practice even for mature
global electronics brands and does not compromise the \'engineered in
India\' positioning, since the value-adding design and integration work
stays domestic.

8.3 Import Initially, Revisit as Volume Grows

Precision LED diodes (Korea/Taiwan), lab-grade pH/EC/CO2/PAR sensors
(USA/Switzerland/China), rockwool (Netherlands), IP-rated connectors,
and camera/edge-AI modules currently have no adequate domestic source at
the required grade. None of these are strategic blockers --- they are
standard, distributor-available components in every country\'s premium
CEA supply chain, including the Netherlands\' and Japan\'s own benchmark
manufacturers.

8.4 A Genuine Cross-Cutting Risk to Flag

The LED diode market note that Samsung exited LED chip production in
2025 is a live supply-chain risk worth designing around now: fixture
specifications should reference \'Epistar/Ennostar or equivalent\'
performance targets rather than a single named chip family, so that a
future supplier exit does not force a fixture redesign.
